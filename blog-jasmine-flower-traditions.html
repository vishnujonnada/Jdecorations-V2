Jasmine Flowers in Indian Pujas & Traditions

January 22, 2026 | 5 min read | By JDecorations Team | Floral Design, Puja Decor

Jasmine flowers, commonly known as Mogralu or Jaji Malli, are deeply woven into Indian spiritual and cultural traditions. Their soothing fragrance and pure white appearance symbolize devotion, peace, and prosperity.

Religious Significance of Jasmine Flowers

Jasmine is strongly associated with Goddess Lakshmi and Goddess Andal. It is considered highly auspicious and is widely used in temples and household pujas to invite divine blessings and positive energy.

Occasions Where Jasmine Is Traditionally Used

  • Varalakshmi Vratham
  • Wedding muhurtham ceremonies
  • Temple offerings
  • Naming ceremonies (Barsala)
  • Daily home pujas
Decoration Tip: Use jasmine garlands for idols and combine them with roses or marigolds for a balanced traditional look.

Jasmine in Wedding & Hair Decoration

Jasmine gajras are an essential part of South Indian bridal styling. They enhance the bride’s look while spreading a calming fragrance throughout the ceremony.

“The fragrance of jasmine creates a calm, sacred, and divine atmosphere.”

Conclusion

Jasmine flowers bring unmatched elegance and spiritual depth to Indian pujas and celebrations. Their timeless beauty makes them an irreplaceable element in traditional decoration.

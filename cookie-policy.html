Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies on our website

Last Updated: December 30, 2024

This Cookie Policy explains how JDecorations ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website jdecorations.in. This policy should be read in conjunction with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide information to website owners, and improve your browsing experience.

Key Characteristics of Cookies:

  • Stored in your web browser
  • Can remember information about your visit
  • Help websites recognize returning visitors
  • Enable personalized experiences
  • Can be deleted or blocked by users

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential/Strictly Necessary Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. Without these cookies, certain services cannot be provided.

Examples:

  • Session cookies that remember your navigation through the site
  • Security cookies for secure transactions and authentication
  • Load balancing cookies for site performance

Can you refuse them? No, these are necessary for the website to work.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Purpose: These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, helping us improve functionality and user experience.

What we track:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Navigation patterns and user journey
  • Error messages encountered
  • Browser and device information
  • Geographic location (country/city level)

Third-party provider: Google Analytics

Can you refuse them? Yes, you can opt-out using browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure advertising campaign effectiveness.

What they do:

  • Display relevant ads based on your interests
  • Limit the number of times you see an ad
  • Measure ad campaign effectiveness
  • Remember your ad preferences

Third-party providers: Google AdSense, DoubleClick

Can you refuse them? Yes, you can opt-out at Google Ads Settings or through your browser settings.

Functionality Cookies

Purpose: These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide enhanced, personalized features.

What they remember:

  • Language preferences
  • Region or country selection
  • Customized settings
  • Previous form inputs

Can you refuse them? Yes, but this may affect website functionality and your user experience.

4. First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies

First-Party Cookies

These are cookies set directly by our website (jdecorations.in). They help us:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Track your session
  • Improve website functionality

Third-Party Cookies

These are cookies set by external services we use, such as:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics and performance tracking
  • Google AdSense: For displaying relevant advertisements
  • Social Media Plugins: If you share our content on social platforms

Third-party cookies are subject to the privacy policies of these external services.

5. Session vs. Persistent Cookies

Session Cookies

  • Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Help with navigation and remembering information during your visit
  • Do not collect information from your device

Persistent Cookies

  • Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • Remember your preferences for future visits
  • Help provide a personalized experience

6. How to Manage and Delete Cookies

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Accept only first-party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites
  • Get notified when cookies are being set

Browser-Specific Instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies
  • Opera: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies

Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on
  • Google Ads: Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative: NAI Opt-out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: DAA Opt-out
Important: Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website. Some features may not function properly, and you may need to manually adjust some preferences on each visit.

7. Mobile Device Cookie Management

For mobile devices, cookie management varies by operating system and browser:

iOS (iPhone/iPad)

  • Settings > Safari > Block All Cookies
  • Settings > Safari > Advanced > Website Data (to remove existing cookies)

Android

  • Browser app > Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data
  • Select "Cookies and site data" and clear

8. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you don't want your online activity tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals. We respect your privacy choices and encourage you to use the cookie management options outlined above.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through cookies or any other means. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us immediately.

Expert Guide to Choosing Wedding Flowers for Different Seasons

December 15, 2024 | 8 min read | By JDecorations Team | Floral Design, Seasonal Flowers

Flowers are the soul of Indian weddings, but choosing the right blooms depends heavily on the season. In Andhra Pradesh's tropical climate, availability, freshness, and pricing vary dramatically throughout the year. Selecting seasonal flowers ensures better quality, lower costs, and stunning results.

At JDecorations in Kakinada, we work with local flower markets daily and have perfected the art of seasonal floral design. Here's your complete guide to the best wedding flowers for every season, complete with pricing, care tips, and beautiful alternatives.

Share this article:

🌸 Winter Weddings (Nov-Feb) - Peak Flower Season

Best time for weddings! Cool weather means abundant, long-lasting blooms at reasonable prices.

Roses

₹200-400/kg | Red, white, pink - perfect for mandaps & garlands

Marigolds

₹50-100/kg | Classic yellow/orange for torans & backdrops

Tuberose (Rajnigandha)

₹300-500/kg | Fragrant white flowers for evening ceremonies

Carnations

₹150-250/kg | Long-lasting filler flowers in every color

☀️ Summer Weddings (Mar-Jun) - Heat-Resistant Blooms

Focus on hardy flowers that withstand Kakinada's summer heat and humidity.

Marigolds

₹80-150/kg | Heat champion - available all summer

Jasmine (Mallepuvvu)

₹400-800/kg | Local favorite, fragrant, evening weddings

Chrysanthemums

₹100-200/kg | Vibrant colors, good shelf life

Zinnia

₹80-150/kg | Bold summer colors, budget-friendly

Pro Tip: Schedule summer deliveries for early morning. Use misting sprays to keep flowers hydrated during hot afternoons.

🌧️ Monsoon Weddings (Jul-Oct) - Humidity Lovers

Tropical flowers thrive in monsoon humidity. Avoid delicate imported blooms.

Lotus

₹500-1000 each | Sacred, stunning for mandaps

Hibiscus

₹50-100/dozen | Bright red, long-lasting in humidity

Gladiolus

₹200-400/dozen | Tall spikes perfect for stage arrangements

Cockscomb (Plume)

₹60-120/kg | Unique texture, vibrant colors

Flower Care Tips for All Seasons

  • Storage: Keep at 10-15°C, high humidity (80-90%). Never refrigerate tropical flowers
  • Hydration: Cut stems at 45° angle, change water every 6 hours
  • Transport: Use wet newspaper wrapping for garlands, mist regularly
  • Preservation: Sugar + bleach solution extends life by 24-48 hours

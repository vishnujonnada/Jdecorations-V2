Marigold Flower Decoration in Indian Pujas & Traditions

January 20, 2026 | 6 min read | By JDecorations Team | Floral Design, Puja Decor

Marigold flowers, popularly known as Banthi Poolu, are an integral part of Indian religious and cultural celebrations. Their vibrant yellow and orange shades symbolize positivity and divine blessings.

Spiritual Significance of Marigolds

Marigolds are believed to attract positive energy and are commonly offered to Lord Ganesha, Goddess Lakshmi, and Lord Hanuman during pujas.

Occasions Where Marigolds Are Used

  • Ganesh Chaturthi
  • Navratri & Dasara
  • Wedding mandaps
  • Housewarming ceremonies
  • Temple festivals
Pro Tip: Combine marigold garlands with mango leaves for a classic South Indian puja entrance.
“Marigolds instantly make any space feel festive, sacred, and joyful.”

Conclusion

Marigold flower decoration remains timeless in Indian traditions. Its affordability, beauty, and spiritual value make it irreplaceable.
